## 📌 Course & Repository Overview
This repository contains coursework, lab assignments, and term project deliverables for **INFO 450: Programming for Business Analytics** at VCU. 

### VCU Official Course Description
> *"This course is designed to provide immediately useful skills that can be applied in everyday business activities and technology investments, especially in AI and analytics systems. It covers the design and implementation of a range of emerging technologies, surveying methods, tools, and techniques required to successfully create and field systems incorporating AI and data analytics while fostering a deeper understanding of their strategic implications in business."*
